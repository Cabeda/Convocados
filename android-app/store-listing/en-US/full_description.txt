Convocados runs your sports group: events, attendance, teams, scores and payments in one place, for the people you actually play with.

Create the event, invite the group, take attendance, split the teams, keep the score and settle what each player owes — on your phone, or on your watch when the game is on.

WEAR OS APP
Convocados for Wear OS is a standalone app, not an accessory. Sign in with Google on the watch, or let your phone sign in for you.

- Quick game tile: add the "Quick game" tile to the tile carousel on your watch. When no game is running it shows a Start button; while a quick game is live it shows the running score and the sport. Tap it and you land in the game, not on a list.
- Live score on the watch face: while a game is being scored, Convocados posts an ongoing activity. The watch face shows the live indicator and the running score, and the recent-apps chip takes you back into the match with one tap.
- Quick game: start a match in seconds and score it on the watch. Tennis, padel or a plain match, with sets and a tiebreak when you need one.
- Your games on your wrist: keep the score, split the teams, add players, vote MVP and browse the history of finished games.
- Game alarm: a distinctive triple-pulse vibration before kickoff, so you are not the one who arrives late.
- Works with no coverage: the watch queues changes offline and syncs them when you are back online.
- Round and square watches: the layout adapts to the shape of your watch.

Scores you keep on the watch sync back to the event on your phone.
